Verify a paper

A completed review classifies findings as minor (a released repair suggestion fixes the issue completely) or major (no complete released fix), audits those classifications, and produces a template PDF (up to four additional Codex calls). PDF output requires XeLaTeX or LuaLaTeX installed locally.

Upload or paste one paper (LaTeX or Markdown). The question to referee is prepared from it according to the mode, then the verifier reviews the full paper against that question. It does not edit the paper. Paper review reports findings and coverage, without an overall accept/reject verdict. It continues through the selected scope even after finding a serious issue.

Each label must sit inside a theorem-like environment, e.g. \begin{theorem}\label{thm:main} … \end{theorem}. No model call is needed for this mode.

Prove a conjecture

A generation session drafts a proof, the verifier reviews it, and only exact audited feedback goes back to the generator for revision. This repeats until the verifier reaches READY_FOR_HUMAN or the round budget runs out. A handoff still needs a human to read the proof.

Betaversion …

        
Combines Rethlas, an agentic framework for automated mathematical reasoning, with OSVerify, an agentic framework for one-sided verification, running on Codex by OpenAI. It can autonomously attack precisely stated mathematical conjectures, and report imprecisions in a mathematical paper.

Purpose

    
This desktop app is for mathematicians who want to try more advanced AI tools in mathematical research without spending much time on computer science or engineering. For more serious use, the source code and its terminal interface are recommended: they are more stable, more up to date, and more customizable.

    
The desktop app is developed by Shurui Liu, mostly vibe-coded with Claude Code Fable 5.1, and built upon an early adaptation of OSVerify with Rethlas by Fan Nie. It currently supports Codex and macOS only; more will be added if needed.

    
Feedback, comments, and suggestions are very welcome.

Suggestions for users

    
    
      
  1. You need a GPT subscription. Everything stays on your computer: settings, every run with its inputs, reports, and execution records. Model calls go to OpenAI through the Codex CLI under your own account. Rethlas-OSV stores no credentials and sends nothing anywhere else.
    2. 
      
  2. Use it responsibly. People hold different views on the future of mathematics and its protocols, but please make sure you intend to do good for the mathematical community and for humanity.
    3. 
      
  3. Monitor the usage on your GPT account. An agentic workflow can be expensive, since it calls the base model continuously.
    4. 
      
  4. Attribute credit properly when generating proofs. A generated solution may not properly credit previous work or ongoing work in the community.
    5. 
      
  5. Get permission before reviewing a paper. Ask the authors, the journal, and any other party involved. Rethlas-OSV only reports detected correctness issues; it makes no judgement of novelty or significance.
    6. 
      
  6. No guarantee of correctness. Like any AI tool in mathematics, Rethlas-OSV can be wrong. READY_FOR_HUMAN is a handoff for a human reader, not a proof certificate.
    7. 
      
  7. Be transparent about generative AI usage. Judge with common sense.
    8. 
      
  8. We deeply appreciate it if you could cite our papers (in the case that Rethlas-OSV helped your work). The entries below are ready to copy.
